Important Production Notes

  1. Use the supplied official logo as the source of truth. Do not redraw or distort the foundation emblem or wordmark for official communications.
  2. Keep mascot identity consistent: orange five-petal flower head, friendly face, green jacket, orange accents, cream/white clothing, and the orange flower emblem on the jacket.
  3. Check campaign text carefully. PMSBY, PMJJBY and APY names and wording should be proofread before publication.
  4. Check each downloaded file. Some assets were generated as visual concepts or resized exports. Do not assume every file is a native 4K render or a verified transparent cutout.
  5. Review logo legibility. Small text on a mascot tablet, sign, or promotional graphic may need manual correction.
  6. Keep the original master files. Use clearly named copies for resized, compressed, or platform-specific exports.
